# Checked handoffs across structured investigation sessions

The intended outcome was to let a corridor exceed one investigation context
without silently losing evidence or resetting its authorization. The accepted
offline milestone demonstrates that workflow through application: fresh
investigators leave service-checked handoffs, the editor verifies their union,
and the existing review and closeout sequence produces exactly the approved
files. The [acceptance record](../structured-pass/MULTI_SESSION_NATIVE_CHECKPOINT.md)
preserves the result and the earlier stopped attempt.

## What worked

Coverage is tied to frozen source and fact identities. Delivery before the
last compaction cannot establish coverage, and partial checkpoints do not
claim the whole partition. Fresh contexts share the fixed plan's deadline and
counters; they do not create a new retry allowance. The lean edit view supplies
the identities and preimages needed for mutation while keeping full records
available.

The final recipe exercised the real native clients, tmux launcher, watchers,
one-use session claims, supervisor and pair application path against a
disposable repository. Three investigation turns and five editing/review turns
completed in 77.5 seconds, with 122 facts calls. Both write checks accepted,
the four committed files matched their preview, and cleanup left nothing
running. Independent review confirmed these claims from the captures and Git
objects. No provider calls or real credentials were used.

## What caused rework

Early prices omitted some delivered assignment and prompt bytes. Binding the
gate to the assembled inputs exposed that difference before more integration
work. The lean edit view reduced redundant data, but conservative estimates
still decided a narrow refusal. A separately authorized measurement then
provided format rates. Its diagnostic pages also showed that the previous
fallback rate understated some responses. The owner fixed Rule 2 before the
next calculation, applying favorable and unfavorable measurements together.

The first full native sequence closed successfully but stopped before
application because the private write rules omitted a reviewer receipt the
adapter deliberately writes. The failed attempt was preserved. A direct
`apply_closed` regression and a freshly sealed receipt rule preceded the next
owner-authorized recipe, which passed through application. Testing a direct
service apply had not covered the pair launcher's additional checks.

These are two reusable lessons: charge the exact delivered artifacts before
building around a capacity estimate, and make the positive acceptance case use
the same launch and application path as production. Refusal controls alone
cannot prove that a correct run is admitted.

## Limits and next evidence

The responses and quality wrappers were scripted. Read receipts establish
delivery and identity, not understanding; a synthetic application proves no
real naming decision. Native token counters in this recipe are synthetic, so
neither cost savings nor real context use can be inferred. Net write snapshots
also do not constitute continuous tracing.

The actual planned editor clears its margin by 1,112 tokens and the tightest
investigator by 339 under the fixed policy. The live workflow must retain
those gates and caps. Splitting editing, rebuilding facts after publication
and continuing review in another fresh context remain deferred.

The next tangible outcome remains one independently reviewed real corridor
commit produced entirely through the structured process. First come
publication review and both CI platforms, then fresh preparation and capacity
prices on merged code. A separate reviewed packet and owner grant are required
for the live attempt. This retrospective authorizes no additional runs or
implementation slices.
